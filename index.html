About 01

I am an Assistant Professor of Geography in the Department of Arts, School of Liberal Arts and Sciences, at Mody University of Science and Technology, Rajasthan, where I conduct research in renewable energy assessment, geospatial modeling, natural resource management and sustainable development.

I completed my doctoral studies in Geography at Hemvati Nandan Bahuguna Garhwal University (a Central University), Uttarakhand. My dissertation examined the geospatial assessment of solar power potential for sustainable development in Tehri Garhwal district, Uttarakhand, using remote sensing and GIS methodologies.

My research has been published in Scopus-indexed and UGC-CARE listed journals, spanning solar energy potential assessment, forest fire risk zonation, watershed prioritization, morphometric analysis using geospatial technologies, and human–wildlife conflict. Through my research and teaching, I work toward evidence-based approaches to environmental challenges and sustainable resource management.
